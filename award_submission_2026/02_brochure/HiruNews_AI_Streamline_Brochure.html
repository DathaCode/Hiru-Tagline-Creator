The Problem & The Solution

HiruNews AI Streamline
The Problem
Sri Lanka's leading television news broadcast, Hiru News, runs three live bulletins daily — at 11:55 AM, 6:55 PM, and 9:55 PM. Each bulletin contains approximately 20 news segments requiring up to 80 separate broadcast tagline PNGs. Before HiruNews AI Streamline, producing these taglines required a fully manual, multi-team relay: reporters submitted raw material; transcriptionists converted audio to text by hand; writers drafted Sinhala taglines; editors reviewed and returned corrections; a separate graphics team of 8 members created PNG overlays in design software; and completed assets were reviewed again before playout. Every handoff introduced delay. Every correction cycle consumed critical minutes — minutes that, during national emergencies like floods, cyclones, and landslides, could determine whether vulnerable Sri Lankan citizens received warnings before or after danger arrived.

The Solution — One Unified Pipeline
HiruNews AI Streamline collapses the entire relay into a single AI-assisted production pipeline. Module 1 ingests raw press recordings, performs AI-powered transcription with speaker diarisation, and generates complete Sinhala tagline sets — all routed through a mandatory senior editor approval gate. Module 2 receives approved content and renders broadcast-ready PNG overlays using AI-powered layout intelligence that automatically scales, fits, and balances every Sinhala glyph to the template. One journalist now takes raw material from intake to broadcast-ready output — with no graphics team roundtrip — while editorial oversight remains intact at every stage. The AI suggests. The journalist decides. Nothing reaches the air without a human saying yes.

Core Features — Both Modules

HiruNews AI Streamline
Module 1 AI News Processing Engine
📡
Multi-Format Source Ingestion
Accepts audio (MP3, WAV, M4A), video (MP4), and documents (TXT, DOC, PDF). Drop files directly into the web interface. Bulletin metadata — reporter, location, time slot, priority — tagged at upload.
🎙
AI Transcription & Speaker Diarisation
Whisper-powered speech-to-text identifies every speaker, timestamp, and word in Sinhala. AI subbing engine corrects grammar and restores proper Sinhala spellings automatically.
✨
AI Tagline Generation
Generates 5 complete tagline sets — Topic Bed headlines, TAG lines, and White Bed text — all in Sinhala, all within exact broadcast word limits. Multiple AI suggestions with word-count validation per line.
✅
Five-Gate Editorial Approval
Facts verified. No defamatory content. Broadcasting standards met. Sinhala grammar reviewed. Source confidentiality preserved. Every gate requires human approval — no automated publishing.
Module 2 Broadcast Graphics Engine — HiruTaglineCreator v3.1
🔤
FM-Font Sinhala Typography Pipeline
762-line deterministic converter: Singlish → Sinhala Unicode → FM ASCII. Handles rakaransaya, yansaya, ර්‍ය conjuncts, word-level exceptions, and Latin PUA with HarfBuzz shaping.
🤖
AI-Powered Layout Intelligence
Every tagline is automatically scaled, fitted, and balanced to the broadcast template — no manual adjustments, no broken kerning, no overflow. The AI reads the canvas and places every glyph precisely.
👁
Live Visual Preview
Real-time broadcast-quality rendering preview. Selected TAG lines cycle through the live canvas. What you see is exactly what goes to broadcast — WYSIWYG at 1920×1080 resolution.
🖨
Selective PNG Generation
Choose individual taglines or batch-generate all. Each PNG is numbered, named, and sequenced for playout. Dual templates — MAIN TAG and SUB TAG — with transparent 1920×1080 overlays.
