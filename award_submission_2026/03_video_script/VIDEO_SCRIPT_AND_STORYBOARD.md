## 3. FULL NARRATION SCRIPT
### (Verbatim — ready for voice recording)

> **Recording note:** Target pace is approximately 130 words per minute. The full script below runs approximately 650 words. Read at a measured broadcast pace — confident and clear, never rushed.

---

**[0:00 — Cold Open]**

"When a cyclone strikes, when floodwaters rise, when every second of warning can save a life — the speed and accuracy of news reaches far beyond information. It becomes a lifeline."

---

**[0:15 — The Problem]**

"For over a decade, Hiru News — Sri Lanka's number one news broadcast — produced every bulletin through a manual chain. Thirty-five staff across three teams: reporters, news writers, and a dedicated graphics team of eight. Every tagline — and a busy bulletin needs eighty — required multiple handoffs, manual transcription, and back-and-forth with the graphics department. In a crisis, this process cost the one thing we could not afford to lose: time."

---

**[0:45 — Introducing the Solution]**

"Lotus Technologies, under Asia Broadcasting Corporation, set out to change this. Led by developer Vidath Dulanjith, we built HiruNews AI Streamline — an AI-powered system that unifies the entire news tagline pipeline, from raw press release to broadcast-ready Sinhala graphic, with human editorial judgment at every stage."

---

**[1:10 — Demo Part 1: AI Processing Module]**

"Here is how it works. Raw news material — audio recordings, video footage, press releases — is uploaded into HiruNews AI Streamline's processing module. The system transcribes the content, identifies individual speakers, and generates Sinhala taglines matched to our broadcast format. But no AI output ever goes to air without human review. A senior journalist verifies every word against our editorial standards — facts, grammar, legal compliance, source confidentiality — before approving the content for production."

---

**[2:00 — Demo Part 2: Broadcast Graphics Module]**

"The approved taglines flow into HiruNews AI Streamline's broadcast graphics engine — in daily production use since August 2025. A single news team member types in Singlish — standard English phonetics — and the system converts it through a seven-hundred-and-sixty-two-rule pipeline into pixel-perfect Sinhala broadcast graphics. No graphics team. No back-and-forth. The same taglines you see rendered here are the ones millions of Sri Lankans see on their screens every day."

---

**[2:50 — Before/After Metrics + Cyclone Impact]**

"The results speak clearly. Sixty-eight percent reduction in production time. Forty-five percent reduction in operational cost. Thirty minutes saved on every bulletin. But the truest measure came in November and December 2025, when Cyclone Ditwa struck Sri Lanka. With HiruNews AI Streamline already in production, Hiru News delivered government evacuation directives, safety warnings, and real-time situation updates to the nation — faster and more accurately than ever before. In a disaster, speed is not efficiency. Speed is safety."

---

**[3:25 — Tech for Good + Social Impact]**

"This is technology built for good. For the mother in a rural village who watches Hiru News because it is the only source she trusts. For the family checking evacuation routes during a cyclone. For the journalist whose confidential sources are protected by on-device processing. And for every citizen who deserves accurate, timely news in their own language. Hiru News has been recognised with over thirty national and international awards — including Outstanding Humanitarian Service for our disaster relief broadcast. HiruNews AI Streamline ensures that commitment continues at the speed our audience needs."

---

**[4:05 — Technology & Future]**

"Built with PyQt5, Gemini Pro, OpenAI, and a locally deployable language model — and developed entirely through AI-assisted agentic coding — HiruNews AI Streamline demonstrates that world-class broadcast technology can be built in Sri Lanka, by Sri Lankan engineers, for Sri Lankan needs. Looking ahead: Tamil and English expansion, a fully on-premise language model for maximum source protection, and a subscription licensing model to bring this technology to newsrooms across South Asia."

---

**[4:35 — Closing]**

"Lotus Technologies. Under Asia Broadcasting Corporation. Powering the news Sri Lanka trusts — faster, smarter, and always with people at the centre."
